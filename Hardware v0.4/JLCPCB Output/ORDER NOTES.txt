What changed from v0.3
  F1/F2 (0R links) are replaced by two TI TPS259470A eFuses:
    U4 on +5 V : cuts the C64 off above 5.70 V (back on below 5.18 V), 3.3 A current limit (R17 1k).
    U5 on 12 V : cuts DIN pin 6 off above 14.2 V (back on below 12.9 V), 1.5 A current limit (R22 2.2k).
  Both block reverse current and retry automatically after a fault.
